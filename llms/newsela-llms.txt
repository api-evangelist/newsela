# Newsela

> Newsela is a content and assessment platform that provides research-backed, engaging, differentiated content and AI-powered tools to support educators and drive student growth.

## Products

- [Products Overview](https://newsela.com/products): Explore all Newsela products designed to engage, support, and grow every learner.
- [Newsela ELA](https://newsela.com/products/ela): Differentiated texts, novel studies, and curriculum complements to support knowledge-rich literacy instruction.
- [Newsela Social Studies](https://newsela.com/products/social-studies): Primary sources, biographies, current events, and interactive multimedia to support critical thinking and diverse perspectives.
- [Newsela STEM](https://newsela.com/products/stem): Engaging videos, and standards-aligned texts, lessons and activities to support real-world science connections.
- [Newsela Writing](https://newsela.com/products/writing): AI-powered, rubric-aligned writing feedback delivered in real time to support writing practice across the curriculum.
- [Balanced Assessment by Formative](https://www.formative.com/balanced-assessment): Unify district assessments and deliver real-time, standards-aligned insights.
- [Schoolytics](https://www.schoolytics.com/): Unify student data in one intuitive platform with role-based dashboards for actionable insights.
- [What's New](https://newsela.com/whats-new): Latest product updates and features.
- [Newsela Add-ons](https://newsela.com/products/add-ons): Additional features and enhancements for Newsela products.
